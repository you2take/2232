どのようなジャンルで活動でき、どのような仕事があるのかを分けて見せています。写真とイラストを添えた説明を読み進めた先に登録フォームを置き、仕事内容の紹介から手続きまでをまとめています。

Explore opportunities with SHELL

A registration page for people interested in joining SHELL as influencers. Photography and illustration introduce the fields it covers and the types of work available. The page separates these explanations into sections before leading to the registration form, letting prospective applicants review the opportunities and then continue with their details.
