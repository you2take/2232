Meet the Smart Stove

A product website for Osaka Gas's Smart Stove introduces the appliance through cooking, hands-on reports and television commercials. Circular photographs bring people and the product together, forming entry points to the content. The page presents both the appliance itself and examples of its use, placing its features in a kitchen context.
